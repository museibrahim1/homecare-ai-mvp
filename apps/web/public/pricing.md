# Pricing - PalmCare AI

PalmCare AI turns a recorded home care assessment into a state-specific service
contract automatically. Subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple
In-App Purchase in the PalmCare iOS app (charged to your Apple ID).

Last updated: 2026-08-27. Currency: USD.

## PalmCare Mobile
- Price: $89.99/month
- Free trial: 30 days
- Assessments: 15 per month
- Clients: up to 30
- Includes: iPhone assessments, web CRM within client caps, AI voice-to-contract,
  SOAP notes and billables, 50-state compliance engine, HIPAA BAA
- Best for: solo operators and small caseloads

## PalmCare Platform
- Price: $199.99/month
- Free trial: 30 days
- Assessments: 30 per month
- Clients: up to 150
- Includes: everything in Mobile, plus higher caps, team seats, pipeline,
  calendar, custom contract templates, priority support, 250 GB storage
- Best for: agencies that need fuller CRM and team seats

## Enterprise
- Price: custom quote
- Free trial: not self-serve (sales quote)
- Includes: custom assessment and client caps, SSO, dedicated success support,
  volume pricing
- Best for: multi-location agencies
- Request a quote: https://palmcareai.com/book-demo

## Notes
- Three options: Mobile ($89.99/mo), Platform ($199.99/mo), Enterprise (quote)
- Free trial: 30 days when you subscribe to Mobile or Platform in the iOS app
- Billing: handled by Apple In-App Purchase; manage or cancel anytime in iPhone
  Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions
- Security: HIPAA-compliant, AES-256 encryption, role-based access, audit logs
- Payments: processed by Apple (In-App Purchase); we never store payment card details
