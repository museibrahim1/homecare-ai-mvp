# Pricing — PalmCare AI

PalmCare AI turns a recorded home care assessment into a state-specific service
contract automatically. Subscriptions are purchased and billed through Apple
In-App Purchase in the PalmCare iOS app (charged to your Apple ID).

Last updated: 2026-08-24. Currency: USD.

## PalmCare Mobile
- Price: $80/month (App Store may show $79.99)
- Free trial: 30 days
- Assessments: unlimited on iPhone (no overage fees)
- Team members: 1 seat
- Storage: 50 GB
- Includes: AI voice-to-contract, smart SOAP notes and billables, 50-state
  compliance engine, HIPAA BAA, iPhone app access
- Best for: solo caregivers and nurses who run assessments on iPhone only

## PalmCare Platform
- Price: $199/month
- Free trial: 30 days
- Assessments: unlimited on iPhone and web (no overage fees)
- Team members: unlimited
- Storage: 250 GB
- Includes: everything in Mobile, plus web CRM, analytics, custom contract
  templates, and priority support
- Best for: agencies that want the full web dashboard and team seats

## Notes
- Two plans: Mobile ($80/mo) for iPhone assessments, Platform ($199/mo) for the
  full agency stack on web and iPhone.
- Free trial: 30 days when you subscribe in the iOS app
- Billing: handled by Apple In-App Purchase; manage or cancel anytime in iPhone
  Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions
- Mobile subscribers can view billing on web; web CRM requires Platform
- Security: HIPAA-compliant, AES-256 encryption, role-based access, audit logs
- Payments: processed by Apple (In-App Purchase); we never store payment card details
