# PALM Manual-First Outbound Playbook

Adopted Sep 2026, based on the YC Startup Podcast episode "8 Ways To Improve
Your Outbound Sales" (Sep 13, 2026). The two lessons this playbook implements:

1. **Do at least 100 outreaches by hand before automating anything.** A blast
   campaign that gets zero replies teaches you nothing, because the failure
   could be messaging, company targeting, prospect selection, deliverability,
   or the subject line. You cannot tell which. Hand-written sends isolate the
   variables one prospect at a time.
2. **Targeting beats perfect messaging.** Hours spent tweaking copy are wasted
   if the person reading it does not have the exact pain. Pick people who are
   drowning in assessment paperwork and contract drafting, and a plain email
   works. Pick the wrong people and no subject line saves it.

## Where we are honestly starting from

We have already run the anti-pattern. `scripts/email/send_all_outreach.py`
picks one of three generic HTML templates by hashing the agency name, opens
with "Hi," (no name), and batch-sends. The older sequence in
`marketing/email-campaigns/cold-outreach-with-screenshots.md` is worse: it
contains invented numbers ("163 agencies across 48 states", a $50,000 Texas
story) that violate our own claims rules. It is now marked deprecated.

Meanwhile, every real conversation we have gotten (Stacie at Comfort Keepers,
Kena at AccentCare, the Fortcom signup) came from an email written for one
specific person. The data we have already agrees with the podcast.

**Rule going forward: no new automated outbound sequences until the 100-send
hand campaign below is complete and has produced a repeatable pattern that
earned replies.** The GitHub Actions social posting stays. Transactional and
digest email stays. New cold blasts stop.

## The target (decide who before what)

PALM's exact-pain buyer:

- **Owner or administrator of a small non-medical home care agency** (roughly
  1 to 3 offices, under ~50 caregivers). At this size the owner still does
  intake visits personally, writes the care plan, and drafts the service
  agreement, usually at night.
- **Agency is young (0 to 4 years old) or actively growing.** New owners have
  no legacy software, no ops person, and feel every hour of paperwork
  themselves. NPI enumeration date and state license date are good proxies.
- **Private-pay or Medicaid-waiver non-medical care.** These agencies sign a
  service contract with every client, which is the document PALM generates
  with state-specific rules.
- Bonus signals: the website mentions "personalized care plans" (they write
  these by hand), the contact email is a person's name rather than info@,
  the owner is active on LinkedIn or Facebook.

Weak fits, deprioritize: franchises with corporate-mandated software (Home
Instead, Senior Helpers corporate offices), large Medicare-certified home
health chains, government-operated agencies, facilities (assisted living).

## The 100-by-hand campaign

Worksheet: `marketing/outbound/first-100-worksheet.csv` (generated by
`scripts/leads/build_hand_research_list.py`). Tier 1 is our 32 hand-collected
NE and IA agencies with websites. Tier 2 fills to 100 with proprietary
agencies from the CMS file in nearby target states. Re-rank freely; the
worksheet is a starting list, not a quota of exactly these names.

Pace: 5 per weekday is 100 in a month. Do not batch 20 in a day; quality
drops and you stop noticing what you are learning.

### The 15-minute homework per prospect (before writing a word)

1. **Find the person, not the agency.** Owner or administrator by name. NPI
   registry (npiindex.com, NPPES) lists the authorized official. State
   license lookups, LinkedIn, the website's About page, Google reviews
   (owners reply to reviews with their name).
2. **Read their website like a customer.** What services do they lead with?
   Do they mention care plans, assessments, service agreements? Any hint of
   how new or big they are?
3. **Check their LinkedIn or Facebook.** What do they post about? Hiring?
   Caregiver appreciation? A webinar? Anything recent is an opener.
4. **Form one specific pain hypothesis.** Not "paperwork is annoying" but
   "she registered the agency in Feb 2025, has no office staff listed, and
   advertises personalized care plans, so she is writing those plans herself
   after visits."
5. **Log it** in the worksheet row before sending: person, detail found, pain
   hypothesis, angle. If you cannot fill in a personal detail, you have not
   done the homework yet.

### Writing the email (rules)

- **Plain text.** From Muse, sales@send.palmtai.com, reply-to
  sales@palmtai.com. No HTML card, no QR code, no screenshots on the first
  touch. It should look like a founder typed it, because he did.
- **Under 120 words.** First line proves you looked at them specifically.
  Never open with "Hi," and never with a line about PALM.
- **One pain, one product fact, one next step.** Product facts we can back:
  one recording becomes four documents (care plan, billables, visit notes,
  contract), contract rules for all 50 states, minutes not hours, live on the
  App Store, 14-day free trial. Nothing else. No invented customer counts,
  no fake stories.
- **Subject line is lowercase, specific, and about them.** "your care plans
  in Elkhorn" beats "Revolutionize your agency workflow" every time.
- Follows the brand voice guide and the no-slop rules: no em dashes, no
  slash-joined alternatives, no buzzwords, no exclamation marks.

### Worked example (real research, done Sep 15, 2026)

Prospect: **Warm Embrace Home Care LLC**, Elkhorn NE (Omaha metro).
Homework found: owner and authorized official is **Sommer Lisenby**; NPI
enumerated **Feb 25, 2025**, so the agency is about 18 months old;
non-medical (in-home supportive care taxonomy); homepage leads with
"personalized care plans designed to meet the specific needs of each client"
plus dementia and respite care; contact email is her name, not info@.
Pain hypothesis: she is doing intake visits herself and writing every care
plan and service agreement by hand at night.

> **To:** sommer@warmembracehc.org
> **Subject:** your care plans in Elkhorn
>
> Hi Sommer,
>
> I saw Warm Embrace leads with personalized care plans, and that you started
> the agency early last year. At that stage I am guessing you are still doing
> the intake visits yourself and writing the plan and the service agreement
> afterward.
>
> I built an iPhone app for exactly that. You record the assessment
> conversation, and it writes the care plan, the visit notes, the billables,
> and a Nebraska service contract from what was actually said. Minutes, not
> the evening.
>
> I am in Omaha too. Want to try it on one visit this week? It is free for 14
> days, and I will personally walk you through it.
>
> Muse Ibrahim
> Founder, PalmCare AI (palmcareai.com)

Why this works as a template for the standard, not the copy: the first line
could not have been sent to anyone else. The pain line is a hypothesis she
can correct (a reply either way). One product fact block. One local, low-risk
next step.

### After sending: the learning log

Every send gets a row in `marketing/outbound/hand-outreach-log.csv` with the
angle used and the result. This is the entire point of doing it by hand. Log:

- **angle**: which pain you led with (care-plan writing, evening paperwork,
  contract drafting, new-agency setup, local founder, follow-up to a call).
- **result**: no_open, open_no_reply, reply_negative, reply_neutral,
  reply_positive, call_booked, signup.
- **learning**: one sentence. "She replied but said contracts are handled by
  her attorney" is worth more than ten opens.

Weekly review (Fridays): sort the log by angle and compare reply rates. After
100 sends you will have real answers to: which segment replies (state, age,
size), which pain line lands, which subject lines get opened, whether
deliverability differs by domain. Those answers, not templates, are what we
are buying with the manual effort.

### Follow-up

One follow-up per prospect, 4 to 6 days later, in the same thread, max three
sentences, adding one new specific thing (a 7-second screen recording, a
state-specific fact, a genuine reaction to something they posted). No
five-email drip. If two touches get nothing, log it and move on; the list is
long.

## The automation gate

Automation is allowed again only when all three are true:

1. 100 logged hand sends are complete.
2. One angle plus segment combination has at least a 10 percent reply rate
   over at least 30 sends.
3. The automated version reuses the exact winning angle and still requires a
   human-written first line per prospect (semi-automated, not blast).

Until then, treat any request to "load the list and send" as out of policy.
