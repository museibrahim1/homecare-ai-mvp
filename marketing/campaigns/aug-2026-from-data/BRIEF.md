## What the data says

### Channel reality
- Instagram carried almost all discovery: **5,727 views / 4,136 reach** across 69 posts.
- Facebook Page organic is near dead: **272 views / 67 reach** across 55 posts, **zero boosted reach**.
- **Reels beat static images ~3x** on IG (avg 200 views vs 65). Lead with short video.

### Winners (double down)
| Rank | Format | Views | Hook pattern |
|------|--------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Reel 12s | 366 | First-person evening relief: "I used to lose every evening to paperwork..." |
| 2-3 | Reel 7-12s | 278-322 | App Store live + record → plan, billables, contract |
| 4 | Reel 20s | 241 | Drive-back rebuild-by-hand problem story |
| 5 | Reel 7s | 224 | Ultra-short pitch: "7 seconds. That's the whole pitch." |
| 7 | Image | 169 | Myth bust: "AI will replace caregivers" is wrong |
| 8 | Image | 163 | Group-chat social moment: contract out before lunch |
| 10 | Image | 136 | Friday leave-on-time humor |

### Losers (retire as primary creatives)
- Pure **"Download PALM today" + QR** cards (3 to 6 views).
- Vague abstract lines with no story ("Same visit. Different evening.", "The visit was already documented...") when used alone.
- Long how-to (66s): gets 3-second taps but average watch ~4s. Keep demos under 15s, or cut hard after the hook.

### Patterns to keep
- First-person caregiver voice.
- Concrete evening / kitchen-table / Friday-clock-out moments.
- One recording → transcript, care plan, billables, contract.
- Myth bust that protects the caregiver's job.
- Short: **7 to 15 seconds** for Reels.

### Patterns to kill
- Unverifiable claims ("number one AI assessment tool"). Dropped from all new copy.
- Emoji-heavy or hype openers.
- QR-first creatives with no story.
- Em dashes, slash-joined alternatives, AI slop (per brand rule).
