# Engagement posts (LinkedIn + Threads)

Approval-only. **Never auto-posted.**

## Voice
Written as Muse. First person. Short sentences. Home care specifics.
No AI filler. No engagement bait. You edit or kill anything before it posts.

Themes come from real founder work (aging care, trust, talk to users, durable workflows).
Wording is original to PalmCare. Nothing copied from YC or anyone else.

## How it works
1. Mon–Fri 8:00 AM ET email with 3 LinkedIn + 3 Threads options.
2. Reply `LI: A` / `TH: B` or paste your rewrite.
3. Only then does an agent post.
4. Product marketing stays on `social-posts.yml`.
